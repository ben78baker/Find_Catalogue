# Find Catalogue privacy policy

**Effective date:** 31 August 2026

**Developer/data controller:** Benjamin Matthew Baker

**Contact:** bmbapps@icloud.com

Published at <https://ben78baker.github.io/find-catalogue-privacy-policy/>. This project copy should be kept in step with the public page. It is practical project wording, not legal advice.

## Summary

Find Catalogue is designed as a private, local-first catalogue. It does not require an account and does not contain advertising, analytics or developer-operated telemetry.

## Information stored on the device

The app can store information that you choose to enter or capture, including:

- photographs of finds;
- precise or manually entered find locations and location accuracy;
- discovery dates;
- identifications, measurements, observations, research notes, sources and storage information; and
- app-generated permanent catalogue numbers.

This catalogue is stored in the app’s local container on your device. The developer does not operate a server that receives or stores the catalogue. Your device or operating-system backup service may include app data according to your own backup settings and the backup provider’s terms.

## Camera, photographs and location permissions

Camera and photo-library access are used only when you choose to take or select a find photograph. Location access is used when you choose Instant Find or request the current location. You can deny these permissions and create records with manually entered or unknown discovery information.

## Interactive maps and OpenStreetMap

Interactive maps use tiles supplied by the OpenStreetMap Foundation. Only when you open a map, the app requests tiles covering the displayed area. These requests disclose the displayed map area and technical connection information, including your IP address and application/device information, to OpenStreetMap and its delivery providers. They do not include your record title, photographs, research notes or catalogue database.

OpenStreetMap handles those requests under its privacy policy:

https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

The app’s catalogue and coordinate entry continue to work without opening an interactive map.

## Sharing and exports

The app shares information only when you start an export and choose a destination through the device’s share interface. Before an export is created, you choose whether exact coordinates are hidden or included. When a photo bundle hides locations, supported photographs are re-encoded without embedded metadata; a file that cannot be sanitised safely is omitted and identified in the bundle manifest.

After you share a file, the receiving person, app or service processes it under their own terms and privacy practices.

## Retention and deletion

Catalogue data remains on the device until it is changed by you or the app is deleted. Deleting the app removes its local app container, subject to copies that may remain in device backups or files that you previously exported. The app has no online account or developer-held server record requiring a separate deletion request.

## Children

The app is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly collect personal information through a developer-operated service.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.
